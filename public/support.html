How do I join my coach?

Your coach sends you a private invitation link. Open it on your phone, create your account, and your plan appears straight away.

How do I connect my wearable?

Go to You → Connected devices. WHOOP and Google Health (Fitbit Air, Pixel Watch and Fitbit) connect with your account sign-in. On iPhone, Apple Health connects with your permission and syncs automatically. You can also enter readings manually.

What does my coach see?

Your coach sees your coaching records and the wearable readings you share. Under You → Connected devices you can choose whether to share sleep, recovery and activity, and change this at any time.

Are my photos private?

Yes. Progress photos, technique videos and voice notes can be opened only by you and your coach.

How do I delete my account?

Open Account → Delete account, enter your password and type DELETE. This permanently deletes your account and your records. It can’t be undone.

I forgot my password
