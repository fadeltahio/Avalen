Privacy policy

Last updated: [date]

Avalen is a coaching app that connects you with your coach. It is operated by [operator legal name] (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information Avalen handles, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at [privacy contact email].

Who can see your information

Avalen is built around a private workspace between a coach and their clients. Your coach can see the coaching records you create in Avalen, such as workouts, check-ins, messages, measurements and the wearable readings you choose to share. Other clients cannot see your information. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash.
  • Profile: your name, goal and, if you add it, your mobile number.
  • Intake answers: the information you give your coach when you start, which can include health questions.
  • Coaching records: workouts and sets, food and nutrition logs, habits, body measurements and body-composition results, check-in answers, and messages with your coach.
  • Photos, videos and voice notes you choose to add, for example progress photos on a check-in, technique videos or voice updates.
  • Wearable and health readings, only if you connect a source: sleep, recovery, strain, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, steps, calories and similar readings from WHOOP, Google Health (Fitbit and Pixel Watch), Apple Health or Health Connect, or readings you enter yourself.
  • Payment records: the amount, date, status and method of payments to your coach. Card payments are processed by Paymob; we never receive your full card number. We may keep the last four digits shown on the receipt.
  • Technical information: a sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in. Avalen does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To provide the coaching service: showing your plan, recording your progress and letting you and your coach communicate.
  • To show your coach the wearable readings you choose to share, so they can adjust your training.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent misuse and fix problems.

Health information, including data from Apple Health and Health Connect, is used only to provide the coaching features you use. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties for those purposes.

Wearables and health data

Nothing is read from a wearable or health app until you connect it and grant permission. You choose which groups your coach can see (sleep, recovery and activity) and can change this at any time under You → Connected devices. You can disconnect a source at any time; when you disconnect WHOOP or Google Health, we ask that provider to revoke Avalen’s access, and you can choose to delete past readings. Wearable scores are estimates from your device, not medical measurements, and Avalen never changes your plan automatically based on them.

Who we share it with

  • Your coach, as described above.
  • Service providers that run Avalen for us, such as our hosting provider [hosting provider], under agreements that protect your information.
  • Paymob, when you pay your coach online, to process the payment.
  • WHOOP and Google, only when you connect them, so Avalen can fetch your readings.
  • Open Food Facts, when you scan a barcode: the barcode number is sent to look up the food. No personal information is sent.
  • Optional AI or transcription providers, only if your coach’s workspace has them enabled and you give explicit consent, for example to transcribe a voice update.
  • Authorities, if required by law.

Security and storage

Information travels over encrypted connections. Access tokens for connected wearables are encrypted at rest and never sent to your device. Photos, videos and voice notes are private and can be opened only by you and your coach. Uploaded media is kept for up to [365] days unless deleted sooner. Your information is stored in [hosting region].

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account at any time in the app: Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and your coaching records, photos, voice notes and wearable readings. Your coach keeps payment records without your personal details, where needed for their accounting.
  • Change what your coach sees from wearables, or disconnect them, under You → Connected devices.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your information, or to object to its use. Contact us at [privacy contact email].

Children
